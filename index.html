03 / The journey

From learning the craft to shaping the outcome.

Every chapter of my career has added a new dimension to how I approach leadership, people and delivery. What started with learning the fundamentals evolved into managing complex delivery environments, leading teams and finding better ways to solve business problems.

JUL 2024 TO PRESENT

Ernst & Young (EY GDS)

Associate Manager (Senior Consultant 3) · Transformation Delivery

At EY, the scope of my responsibilities expanded beyond delivery management. I took ownership of enterprise transformation engagements, worked closely with senior stakeholders, led cross-functional teams and explored ways to fundamentally improve how work gets done. From shaping global delivery roadmaps and managing complex programme governance to introducing AI-enabled workflows, I began combining execution with innovation, identifying opportunities, creating actionable solutions and translating them into measurable value. This chapter has been about thinking beyond the immediate deliverable: understanding the bigger picture, anticipating what comes next and creating impact through better ways of working.

OCT 2022 TO JUL 2024

TSYS, A Global Payments Company

Scrum Master

At TSYS, I moved from learning the principles of leadership to putting them into practice at scale. Coordinating four teams and orchestrating a group of 30+ people meant balancing delivery expectations, team capacity, dependencies and competing priorities. I embraced servant leadership, focused on creating alignment and learned that execution is as much about managing people and situations as it is about meeting commitments. It was a defining phase in developing my ability to manage complex delivery environments, negotiate priorities and guide teams towards shared outcomes.

JUL 2019 TO OCT 2022

Accenture

Scrum Master

My early career was about understanding how to approach unfamiliar situations, navigate challenges and find solutions when things didn't go as planned. I began in quality assurance, but my curiosity pushed me to look beyond my immediate responsibilities. I took the initiative to learn from an experienced Scrum Master, gradually building my understanding of Agile practices, team dynamics and delivery. It was also where I discovered the importance of taking initiative, supporting people and finding ways to keep teams motivated even in challenging circumstances. This chapter built the foundation for how I would approach leadership in the years ahead.
